# R02 — Notify Autopilot Reached (output)

**Modalities:** Screen and Audio-out

## Function
F-02.2 — Sustain awareness of autopilot conformance (continuous status): the HMI's purpose is to keep the pilot aware of whether the autopilot has reached and is still holding the commanded target parameters, signalling both the 'reached' event and any subsequent deviation from target.

## Behaviour
Be-02.2.3 — Mixed pathway (Screen + Audio-out together): on reaching the target the on-screen status indicator turns green AND an audio 'PLIM!' sounds simultaneously; while conforming it stays green quietly; if the autopilot later drifts off target the indicator flashes AND a warning beep sounds. Screen and audio reinforce each other so the pilot sees and/or hears the notification regardless of head-out or high-noise conditions.

## Structure
S-02.2.3.3 (meio-termo/compromise version) — Autopilot status symbol shown on the HUD (forward windscreen) AND on the WAD (large colour display): green when target reached, flashing when off target; plus cabin Audio-out alto-falante giving a simple 'PLIM!' on reaching target and a beep on deviation (no continuous voice callouts, voice reserved for essential events only).
